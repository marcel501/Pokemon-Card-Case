PROJECT BRIEF
==============

# INTRODUCTION

The project consists of a design effort to develop a card holder case with aesthetic elements which complement the franchise.




## BACKGROUND

As a design engineer your job is to produce a playing cards case. The product must be fully functional which allows the end user to store playing cards securely. The product must involve an aesthetic appeal which includes elements complementing the franchise. In addition, it is required to design the product in 2 different design representations (B-rep & CSG) and 2 different CAD packages.





___

# KEY FEATURES

- Must have a hinged lid design.
- Include a prominent logo of the franchise.
- Must feature the colors of the franchise.
- Must have a snug-fig compartment for the cards - they will be fitted horizontally, planar to the left/right.
